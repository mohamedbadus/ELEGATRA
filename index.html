What we handle

A chandelier is a ceiling job before it is a light

Buying the fixture is the easy half. We do the part that keeps it on the ceiling and keeps it looking new.

Ceiling survey & load rating

We check the slab, the hook and the true weight before quoting. If the ceiling needs a backing plate, that is in the quote, not a surprise on install day.

Wiring, dimming & drivers

Matched dimmers and drivers so the light fades smoothly instead of flickering or dropping out at the bottom of the range.

Assembly and installation

Our own crew assembles on site, hangs the frame, then dresses every crystal by hand. Packing and debris leave with us.

Cleaning & restoration

Annual cleaning, rewiring, and replacement of chipped drops — including fixtures we did not supply.

How it runs

From first visit to first switch-on

  1. 1

    Consultation

    Come to the showroom or send photos of the room. We talk ceiling height, table size and how bright you actually want it.

  2. 2

    Site survey

    We measure the drop, check the fixing point and the existing circuit. Usually one hour.

  3. 3

    Selection & quote

    A fixed written quote covering fixture, dimming, fixing and labour. Valid 30 days.

  4. 4

    Installation

    Delivery and install on an agreed date. Most fixtures go up in a single day.

  5. 5

    Aftercare

    Two years on workmanship, plus a cleaning visit whenever you want one.
